02 · one wish, every year

Not just this Diwali.

I keep thinking about all the festivals still waiting for us. Diwalis with rows of little diyas, Holis with colours everywhere, Christmas lights, New Year countdowns, and all the ordinary evenings in between.

I want the togetherness in all of it. Different seasons, changing traditions — but the same two people making new memories together, year after year.

A note from your favourite person

To my beautiful Diya ❤️

Happy Diwali, my love. 🪔❤️

I can already picture us celebrating together — lighting diyas, watching the sky burst into colours, stealing sweets from the box, and probably annoying you with a hundred pictures of the decorations. I am so excited for all the little moments we are going to make tonight.

And while we celebrate together, I hope you know that the best part of every festival will always be having you there. When I think about Diwali, I don't just picture the lights or the celebrations. I picture your smile, your laughter, and all the little things that make every moment with you special.

You are my favourite kind of magic — the kind that makes an ordinary evening feel special. I hope you never forget just how stunning, rare, and wonderfully you you are. I am ridiculously lucky to celebrate you, too.

I want this to be one of many Diwalis we celebrate together. I want the messy rangolis, the late-night sweets, the family chaos, the tiny traditions that become ours, and all the festivals we haven't even imagined yet.

Here's to growing together, making a home feel warmer, collecting memories in every season, and choosing each other through all the years ahead. May we never run out of reasons to celebrate — or reasons to be grateful that we have each other.

Happy Diwali, my favourite baddie. May every light remind you that you are loved, today and on all the days that follow.

Forever your Nirmal 🫶🏻❤️
